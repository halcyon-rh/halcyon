<h1 align="center">Halcyon</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Sell options onchain. Get paid first.</b></p>

---

Halcyon is an onchain options venue. You write covered calls and cash-secured puts on the crypto and tokenized stocks you already hold. Professional market makers compete to quote you, the premium lands in your wallet upfront, and the whole trade settles in a single transaction.

> **Status:** private beta. Join the waitlist for early access.

## How it works

1. **Pick an asset and a side.** Choose what you hold (BTC, ETH, SOL, tokenized stocks and more) and whether you want to sell a call or a put.
2. **Desks compete for your trade.** Your request goes out as an RFQ. Market makers send firm quotes and you see the best price.
3. **Get paid instantly.** Accept a quote and the premium is in your wallet right away. Your collateral sits in escrow until expiry.

## Why Halcyon

- **Premium upfront.** You earn the moment the trade fills, not at expiry.
- **Competitive pricing.** Multiple desks bid on every request, so you get the best quote available.
- **Atomic settlement.** Collateral lock and premium payment happen in one onchain transaction. No counterparty risk on the payment.
- **Self-custody.** Your assets stay in an onchain escrow, never on an exchange.
- **Crypto and real-world assets.** Earn yield on BTC, ETH, SOL, liquid staking tokens and tokenized stocks.
- **Multichain.** Built for Solana and EVM.
